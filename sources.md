## Editorial rules for the routine

- Split each daily digest roughly into thirds: AI/ML, Uruguay local/economics, international/geopolitical. Keep the AI/ML share capped at about a third — it is context, not the main event.
- AI/ML section: focus on product/business impact — how project and product leaders are using AI to augment delivery today, what's shipping, what tools/vendors are moving. Skip deep model-architecture internals (that's what the weekly deep-dive curriculum is for).
- Uruguay section: prioritize economics and broadly relevant national news (policy, business, major public-interest stories). Explicitly exclude sports.
- International section: geopolitical and globally significant news — what any informed person should know, not niche trade press.
- Rank by significance, not hype or press-release volume. Prefer primary sources over coverage of them where practical.
- If an AI/ML story is methodologically new (architecture, training method, agentic pattern) or a delivery/consulting story raises a durable lesson, flag it as a deep-dive candidate at the end of the digest.
